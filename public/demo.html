The flaw in blanket discounting

Not every over-budget cart deserves a discount.

When an autonomous buyer arrives with a hard budget and the cart is over it, the reflex is to cut price until it fits. On Indian retail margins that reflex quietly destroys the contribution it was meant to protect.

01

Margin sold below cost

A flat percentage is applied without reference to unit cost. A ₹4,500 shoe costing ₹2,700 discounted to ₹3,000 books revenue and loses ₹300 a unit.

02

Free money left unspent

Brand campaigns, bank rail offers and card-network cashback are already funded by someone else. Cutting your own price first spends margin you never needed to touch.

03

Discount stacking with no ceiling

Without a daily release budget and a per-buyer cooldown, the same shopper can be rescued repeatedly and the day's giveaway has no upper bound.

04

An agent that can spend

If the language model is in the authorisation path, a prompt injection is a payment instruction. Intent parsing and money movement have to be separate systems.

Governance

Autonomous where it is safe. Bounded everywhere else.

The language model reads intent and writes explanations. It is nowhere in the authorisation path. Every control below is deterministic, inspectable and enforced server-side.

Mandate-bound spend

A parsed intent becomes a signed mandate with a hard cap. The buyer gate compares totals against it arithmetically — no model output can raise it.

Merchant floor margin

Every candidate offer is priced against real unit cost. Anything that lands under the configured floor returns REJECT_FLOOR and is never released.

Daily release budget

Own-cost discounts accumulate against a per-day ceiling and a per-buyer cooldown. When it is spent, the gate refuses regardless of how good the sale looks.

Hash-chained audit ledger

Buyer and merchant each keep an append-only chain; every entry hashes its predecessor and the tip is signed with ed25519. Tampering breaks verification.

Idempotent payment attempts

Charges carry a deterministic idempotency key and retries are bounded to the buyer's allowed rails, so a network retry cannot double-debit.

Cart-drift detection

The cart is hashed at consent. If it changes before capture the session aborts rather than pay for something the shopper never agreed to.
